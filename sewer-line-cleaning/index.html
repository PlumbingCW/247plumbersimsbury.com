If sewage is coming up in a tub, shower or basement floor drain, call the 24/7 line and stop using water right away. Emergency sewer line cleaning in Simsbury, CT means clearing the pipe that carries everything out of the house to the town sewer or to your septic tank. A blockage here backs up the lowest fixtures first, which is why a basement shower or floor drain often shows it before anything upstairs does.

In Simsbury the line may end at a public sewer, or at a private septic system if the property is not sewered. That difference shapes who is responsible and what to do next.

Sewage coming up in the house: the first ten minutes

What to do right now

The goal is to keep more water out of a line that has nowhere to send it and to keep people away from what is already out.

  • Stop every source of water, including the washer and any slow-running toilet.
  • Keep children, pets and bare feet away. Connecticut DPH says sewage carries bacteria and viruses.
  • Turn off power only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Otherwise leave it and tell us.
  • Find the cleanout, often a capped pipe near the basement floor or outside near the foundation, and keep it clear for the plumber.
  • Call the 24/7 line for a same-day or after-hours visit when a crew is available.

Cleanout access: the fastest way into the line

A sewer cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a cable or jet direct entry to the main line. With one, a plumber can work from the basement floor or yard without pulling a toilet. Without one, we may have to go in through a toilet or a roof vent, which takes longer and carries a bit more risk of a mess.

If you can find your cleanout, do not open it yourself during a backup. The line may be full and the cap could release sewage. A house without a cleanout is a good candidate for adding one during the visit, which makes every future cleaning faster.

Is it your lateral or the town main?

Simsbury's guidance is that the pipes inside a building and the laterals running to the main sewer are the property owner's responsibility, and lateral repairs must be done by a licensed plumber. The town asks you to call the Water Pollution Control Facility at 860-658-3258, or 860-777-8756 after hours, and to report visible sewage outside or in the street, since that may be a public main problem.

The practical rule is simple. If only your house is backing up and the street looks normal, the cause is usually on your side. If neighbors are also backing up or sewage shows at a manhole, call the town while we clear your line, because opening the cleanout will not fix a blocked public main.

Homes that are not on the sewer at all

Not every Simsbury property is connected to the public sewer. The town says septic systems are serviced by private companies and fall under the Farmington Valley Health District at 860-352-2333, and the Water Pollution Control Facility can tell you whether an address is in the sewer service area.

If your home is on septic, a slow main drain can mean a tank that needs pumping or a drainfield in trouble, not only a clog. The EPA advises inspecting a tank every 1 to 3 years and pumping every 3 to 5, depending on tank size and household use. Say you are on septic when you call, and we will check the house line and then tell you if the tank is the real problem.

Sewer smell without a backup

A sewer smell in the bathroom or basement often means a dry trap, a failed wax seal or a vent problem. A cracked or open lateral under the slab can also leak gas into the house. If the smell is strong near a floor drain, pour water in the drain to refill the trap and see if it fades.

When the smell persists, a camera run shows whether the line is damaged, which leads to a sewer line inspection. If you smell gas rather than sewage, leave the building and call Connecticut Natural Gas at 866-924-5325 or 911 before anyone calls a plumber.

How we clear a main line

1

Open the line

We locate the cleanout or pull a fixture and check the flow before we put any tool in.

2

Cable the blockage

A sectional cable with the right head cuts through roots, paper or a grease plug.

3

Jet if needed

When the pipe is sound, hydro jetting cleans the wall and keeps the line open longer.

4

Camera the result

A pass with the camera confirms flow and shows any damage that the cleaning exposed.

What cleaning cannot do

Cleaning will not repair a cracked or bellied line, and it will not unblock a public main. If the camera shows a break, we will describe it honestly and lay out what repair or replacement involves. A leak outside the house that is not sewage may point to a hidden water leak instead.

Landlords and shops run into the same main-line issues, and larger buildings with grease are covered under commercial plumbing. Avon, a short drive away, has its own page at plumber Avon CT.

What to tell dispatch

Have these answers ready. You do not need technical words, and a plain description is fine.

  • Your full street address and the village or town, such as Weatogue, Tariffville or Avon.
  • What is happening now, such as a leak, a clog, no hot water or a sewage smell.
  • Whether water is still running and whether you have closed the main shutoff.
  • Any water near outlets, the electrical panel or appliances.
  • Whether the house is on public sewer or septic, and public water or a private well.
  • A callback number in case the call drops.

While you wait for an emergency visit

Do these first

A few minutes of the right action can cut the damage a lot.

  • Close the main water valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the basement.
  • Switch off the water heater if it is leaking, and stop using drains if sewage is rising.
  • Keep everyone out of rooms with standing water and live electrical.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area, and take photos for your insurer.
  • If you smell gas, leave and call Connecticut Natural Gas at 866-924-5325 or 911 first.

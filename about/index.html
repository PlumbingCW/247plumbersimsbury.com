Simsbury 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing service line for Simsbury and nearby towns. Calls go to local plumbers, you hear a clear price before work starts, and same-day appointments are offered when available.

We keep this page plain on purpose. There are no founder stories or job counts here, because what matters when a pipe is leaking is whether the person on the phone can send a plumber and tell you the truth about the job.

What the service is, in plain terms

Think of us as a front door for plumbing help. You call the 24/7 line, a dispatcher listens to the problem and the address, and the job is matched to a plumber who can do that kind of work, and you can check that plumber's license before work starts.

The business exists for homeowners and small businesses who need a straight answer fast. A basement floor drain that is gurgling at midnight and a dripping kitchen faucet on a Tuesday afternoon both belong on this line. We will tell you which one can wait until morning.

What we cover, and where our list stops

Our service list runs from drain cleaning, hydro jetting and sewer line cleaning, repair and inspection to water heater repair, replacement and installation. It also takes in tankless units, leak detection, burst pipe repair, water line repair, repiping, sump pumps, basement waterproofing, boiler repair, toilets, faucets, garbage disposals, general plumbing installation and commercial plumbing. The full list sits on the services page.

Anything outside that list, we say so. We do not respond to gas leaks. If you smell gas, leave the building and call Connecticut Natural Gas at 866-924-5325 or 911 first, because the gas company responds around the clock and a plumber is not the right first call.

Electrical faults, roofing, mold remediation and insurance adjusting are other trades. If a plumbing leak has damaged drywall or wiring, we fix the plumbing and point you to the right trade for the rest.

How to check any plumber's license in Connecticut

Connecticut law says nobody may perform or offer licensed plumbing work without the license, and the licensee must carry it and show it on request. You can confirm status yourself, and you should do this with every contractor, ours included.

  1. Go to the Department of Consumer Protection eLicense site at elicense.ct.gov and choose Lookup a License.
  2. Search by license type, name or business, and look for an ACTIVE or APPROVED status.
  3. Ask which license class the plumber holds and have the credential details written on the estimate. A P-1 is the unlimited plumbing contractor class, while limited classes cover narrower work such as water pumps or sprinklers.
  4. To ask about complaints against a license holder, call the DCP at 1-800-842-2649 with the credential number ready.

Pricing without surprises

We do not post prices on this site. A sewer camera inspection, a tank swap and a pipe repair behind a finished wall differ too much for any number we could print to be honest.

What we promise is a clear price before work starts, with the reason behind it. If the job turns out different once the pipe is open, the plumber stops and explains before adding anything. You can decline, and a fair plumber will not hold that against you.

Insurance is separate from us. A standard homeowner policy generally covers a burst pipe but excludes slow seepage and sewer backup unless you add coverage, so read your policy or ask your agent, and keep photos.

What we ask of you

A few small things make every visit faster and cheaper for you.

  • Know where your main water shutoff is before you need it.
  • Tell dispatch about water near outlets or the electrical panel straight away.
  • Say whether your house is on public sewer or septic, and whether it uses a private well.
  • Take photos of damage before cleanup, if it is safe to do so.

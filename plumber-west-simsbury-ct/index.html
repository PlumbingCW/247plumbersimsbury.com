West Simsbury occupies the west-central part of Simsbury, bordered by Simsbury Center on the east and Weatogue on the south. Route 309 is its main road, and the post office ZIP code is 06092. The census-designated place covers about 4.4 square miles, which is a lot of ground for 936 housing units.

That spread is the reason this page exists. Houses here sit farther apart than in the village centers, and the first useful question on a plumbing call is often what the house is connected to.

Space between the houses

One real estate site lists a median lot size of about 40,946 square feet in West Simsbury, just under an acre, and describes larger homes on two or more acres. It also gives a median year built of 1967 and notes narrow streets and few sidewalks. Those are real estate estimates, so treat them as a rough picture.

Tulmeadow Farm, founded in 1768, still operates here, and the Simsbury Land Trust helped protect much of its farmland. Open land, long driveways and wooded lots mean longer runs of supply and drain pipe between the street and the house, and more outdoor plumbing to protect.

Sewer or septic, well or public water

The Town of Simsbury says not all properties are on the public sewer system. Septic systems are serviced by private companies, not the Town, and both septic systems and private wells fall under the Farmington Valley Health District. Public water in town is not run by the Town, and Aquarion, Connecticut Water and the Tariffville Water Commission are the listed providers.

No public map we found says which West Simsbury streets use which arrangement. That is why we ask. The answer changes a drain call, because a clog in a septic house is not handled the way a clog on public sewer is.

How the answer changes the job

General plumbing differences, not claims about any particular street.

House on public sewer

  • A blockage can sit in the house line, the lateral or the street main.
  • A camera tells you which part is yours to fix.
  • Sewage in the street goes to the Town.

House on septic

  • A drain that backs up may point to the tank or field, not only a pipe.
  • Pumping and tank work are done by private septic companies.
  • Plumbers clear the house side and tell you when the problem lies beyond it.

Long runs

Mention where the water heater sits relative to the farthest bathroom, since distance affects how long hot water takes to arrive.

Jobs that suit a house with space

Hot water that takes forever

A distant water heater or a failing tank can both cause it. Water heater replacement is worth pricing if the tank is also leaking or rusting.

Slow drains in several rooms

When more than one fixture slows at once, the shared line is the suspect. Drain cleaning with a camera shows how far down it goes.

Pinhole leaks and repeat repairs

A pipe that keeps leaking in new places may be worn throughout. Repiping is a bigger job and a quote should compare it with spot repairs.

Pump work is its own trade

If your trouble is the pump deep in a drilled well, say so on the call. That is specialist work in Connecticut, and we will tell you plainly whether we can help or whether a well pump company is the right call.

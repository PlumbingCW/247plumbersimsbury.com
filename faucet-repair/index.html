When water will not stop, close the valve beneath the fixture first. Look under the sink for two small oval or round handles on the supply lines and turn each clockwise. If they are stuck or leak when touched, go to the main shutoff, and call our 24/7 emergency line while the water is off.

Faucet repair in Simsbury, CT means fixing the working parts of a faucet or valve. That includes cartridges, stems, seats, O-rings and aerators, and replacing the whole fixture when the body is worn through. Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and a pressurized leak gets dispatch priority.

A drip is a nuisance. A valve that will not close is a flood. We treat them differently.

Faucet spraying right now: what to do first

Do this while you wait

If a faucet, tub valve or supply line is spraying, the fastest fix is upstream.

  • Turn off the angle stop under the sink, or the stop behind the toilet if that is the fixture.
  • If the stop will not close or leaks around the stem, shut off the main water valve.
  • Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain pressure from the line.
  • Keep water away from outlets and move rugs and electronics out of the way.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell us which valve you closed.

How to shut water off under a sink

Under most sinks, hot and cold lines run to two angle stops, one on each side. Turn them clockwise until snug, which is usually a quarter or a half turn on newer stops and several turns on older ones. Do not force a stuck stop with pliers, because the stem can snap and leave you with no shutoff at all.

If your stops are old, crusted or never moved, ask us to replace them during the repair. A working stop turns a future emergency into a one-minute fix. It is also the part most often skipped during remodels, which is why plumbing installation visits often include new shutoffs.

Simsbury hard water and what it does to faucets

Aquarion's 2025 water quality report for the Simsbury system gives average hardness of 10 grains per gallon, with a range of 4 to 16. Calcium and magnesium scale builds up in aerator screens and inside cartridges, so you see reduced flow or a handle that grinds and sticks. Unscrew the aerator, soak it in vinegar and see if the flow returns.

Not every address is served by Aquarion, since the Town also lists Connecticut Water and the Tariffville Water Commission. If scale is a recurring headache, ask your utility about your hardness or test your tap, and consider a softener. We can discuss hookups under installing a water filter or softener.

Repair or replace the faucet

The EPA says a faucet dripping once a second wastes more than 3,000 gallons a year, and a showerhead leaking 10 drips a minute wastes more than 500. Fixing it matters either way.

Repair

  • Cartridge and O-ring parts are available
  • The finish and body are in good shape
  • You like the fixture
  • The leak is a drip, not corrosion

Replace

  • The body is pitted or cracked
  • Parts for that brand are hard to find
  • The handle threads are stripped
  • You want a new look or a pull-down spray

Outdoor spigots and a Connecticut winter

The National Weather Service's Hartford climate report lists a 1991 to 2020 normal January low of 18.8 degrees F, so an outdoor spigot on an exterior wall can freeze. The Insurance Information Institute advises disconnecting outdoor hoses before cold weather. A hose left on traps water in the spigot, and the ice can crack the pipe behind the wall without any sign until the thaw.

Disconnect the hose before the first hard freeze, shut off the indoor valve that feeds the spigot if there is one, then open the outdoor tap to drain. We can replace a failed spigot or install a frost-resistant model. If a spigot spurts or drips inside the wall when you turn it on in spring, shut off the interior valve and call us.

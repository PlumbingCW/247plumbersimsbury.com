Tankless Water Heater Service in Simsbury, CT

A tankless unit heats water only when a tap opens, so it lasts longer and takes less room. It also needs yearly descaling, the right gas line and a vent sized for it.

A tankless water heater in Simsbury, CT heats water on demand. When you open a tap, a flow sensor fires the burner or elements, and the water passes through a heat exchanger and leaves hot. There is no stored tank to run out or to rust through, which is why DOE says tankless units can last more than 20 years.

That long life depends on care. Simsbury's well-fed water carries dissolved minerals, and tankless heat exchangers have narrow passages that scale can choke. We install, descale, diagnose and, when it makes sense, replace tankless units, and we tell you plainly when an older one is not worth another repair.

Why Simsbury's water makes descaling a habit

Aquarion's 2025 Simsbury Regional water quality report gives an average hardness of 10 grains per gallon, with a range of 4 to 16, from eight local wells. The report notes that hardness is not regulated by EPA or the state health department, so nobody will flag it for you. It will appear instead as scale on the heat exchanger, reduced flow and eventually a fault code.

That makes yearly descaling a sensible routine for gas and electric tankless units on Aquarion water. Your own address may differ, since the Town also lists Connecticut Water and the Tariffville Water Commission, and some houses draw from private wells. Ask your supplier, or have your tap water tested, and set your descaling interval from what you learn plus the maker's manual.

Annual flush and descaling: what actually happens

A proper descale takes the unit offline for a short period, and the steps below are typical.

1

Isolate the unit

Power and gas are shut off, and the service valves on the hot and cold lines are closed so the heater can be flushed without draining the house.

2

Circulate descaler

A small pump moves food-safe descaling solution through the heat exchanger from one service port to the other, dissolving mineral buildup.

3

Rinse and clean

The unit is flushed with clean water and the inlet filter screen is cleared of grit.

4

Check and restart

Gas pressure, flame, vent and condensate drain are checked, and you see the exhaust and temperature readings.

Repair or replace an older tankless unit

A tankless heater is built to outlast a tank, so many are worth fixing. The deciding facts are the heat exchanger and parts supply.

Repair when

  • The unit is well under 20 years old.
  • The fault is an igniter, flame sensor, fan or sensor, or scale that a descale can clear.
  • The heat exchanger is dry and has not been damaged by freezing.
  • Parts for your model are still available.

Replace when

  • The heat exchanger leaks or has cracked.
  • Descaling does not clear repeated overheat or flow codes.
  • Parts are discontinued or the unit is around the 20-year mark DOE cites.
  • You need more capacity. Compare with a fresh replacement before sinking money into repairs.

Gas line and vent sizing before you install

A gas tankless heater burns much more fuel per minute than a tank, even though it fires only on demand. The existing gas line may not deliver enough, because pipe size depends on the run length, the fittings and every other appliance on that line. Resizing the gas piping is licensed plumbing work, and the gas service in town is Connecticut Natural Gas according to the state utility regulator.

Vent materials depend on the model. Condensing units use plastic pipe and need a condensate drain, while non-condensing units use stainless vent. The maker sets maximum lengths and elbow counts, so the termination point on your wall matters. If you smell gas at any time, leave the house and call Connecticut Natural Gas at 866-924-5325 or 911 before calling us.

Recirculation and cold winter incoming water

A tankless heater raises water temperature by a fixed amount at a given flow. In winter the incoming water is colder, so the same unit delivers less flow at the same set temperature. Ask the installer to size the heater for your coldest months, not summer, and tell them how many showers run together. DOE notes that homes with simultaneous hot water use may need two or more units.

Recirculation adds a return line or a crossover valve so hot water waits near the fixtures. It shortens the wait and avoids the cold slug between bursts. The trade-off is added pumping and a more complex install, so ask whether the model you want supports it.

Limits and when to call for urgent help

We cannot repair a unit whose parts the maker no longer sells, and we cannot make an undersized gas line deliver more gas. If your tankless heater is leaking or has stopped heating tonight, the 24/7 line is open, and water heater repair covers the urgent side. If the leak turns out to be in the walls, leak detection can find it.

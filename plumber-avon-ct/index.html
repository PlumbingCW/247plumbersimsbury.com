Pine Grove, Nod Road and Old Farms: Avon's older corners

Pine Grove, the old 7th School District at West Avon Road and Harris Road, holds four well-preserved 19th-century farmsteads and an 1865 Gothic Revival schoolhouse. Farmhouses that old usually carry several generations of pipe, with bathrooms and kitchens added over the decades. Before a remodel in a house like that, a camera look down the main drain shows what the new fixtures will connect to.

Nod Road recalls the name Avon first went by, before it became Northington. The village took shape where the Farmington Canal, opened in 1828, crossed the Talcott Mountain Turnpike. Streets that old tend to have mature trees over old sewer laterals, so a main drain that slows every spring deserves a root check, not another bottle of drain cleaner.

Columbus Circle, once called Farmington Court, and the Ensign-Bickford worker houses on Mountain View Avenue and Enford Street, per the Avon Historical Society, were built as groups. Houses built together age together, so if neighbours keep replacing the same heater or clearing the same drain, take it as a clue about your pipes.

Drain cleaning on sewer and septic properties

One slow sink is a fixture problem. When the tub, the washer standpipe and a toilet all complain at once, suspect the main line. A cable machine clears most soft blockages, and hydro jetting washes the pipe wall when grease or scale keeps bringing the clog back.

We run a camera when a clog returns within months, since the cause is then usually a cracked joint or roots. Pour cooking fat into a can, not the sink, because hot water and dish soap only move grease farther down the line. If sewage stands in finished living space for more than 24 hours, state health guidance says to bring in a professional.

Water heater replacement: tank or tankless

DOE says most storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years and tankless units can pass 20. A leak at the base means the steel shell has failed.

Replace with a tank

  • Lowest upfront cost and the simplest swap in an existing basement.
  • Works with the gas or electric supply you already have.
  • Hot water can run out when several people shower in a row.
  • Sediment builds up, so a yearly flush helps.

Switch to tankless

  • Heats on demand, so there is no standby tank to lose heat.
  • DOE notes homes with simultaneous use may need two units.
  • Gas models often need new venting and a larger gas line.
  • Ask what yearly service it will need.

Before We Arrive

For an active leak or burst pipe in Avon, these four steps limit the damage while the 24/7 line gets someone moving.

1

Find the main valve

On Connecticut Water, the shutoff is usually where the line enters the basement near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then open a low faucet to drain the pipes.

2

Kill power near water

If water is near a panel, outlet or sump pump cord, do not wade in. Shut off that circuit from a dry spot or leave it for us.

3

Clear the floor

Move boxes off a basement floor and lift rugs. In an older stone-foundation basement, watch for slick, uneven footing.

4

Keep the line open

Stop flushing and running the washer if drains are slow. If you are on a private well, switch off the pump breaker and leave your phone free for our callback.
